--- title: Research permalink: /research/ description: "Physics-guided machine learning research by Xin Lan on lake temperature, harmful algal blooms, and metacoupling." intro: >- I develop physics-guided machine learning to understand and predict how freshwater lakes respond to climate change and human activities. My work combines deep learning with process-based lake models and long-term observations, so that predictions stay physically consistent where measurements are sparse. I apply these methods to lake thermal structure and harmful algal blooms, and I use the metacoupling framework to bring in drivers that originate far beyond a lake's watershed. interests: [AI for Science, Machine learning, Limnology, Metacoupling, Telecoupling, Sustainability, Hydrology, Water resources, Harmful algal blooms] ---
